To use the module go to Employees \> Organizational Chart.

The chart can be expanded or collapsed by clicking on the arrows that
appear when the mouse is over a node (employee). Furthermore, clicking
on an employee will redirect the user to the form view of that employee.

Using the search bar, the user can filter the chart by entering the
employee name or job title. To clear the filter, delete all introduced
characters.

HR officers can also print an official chart from Employees > Reporting >
Official Organizational Chart (or the *Official PDF* button of the chart): a
PDF by department and job position with the company legal name and tax id,
issue and validity dates, the reporting lines and a signature block. It only
holds names and job positions, no private data. An employee can be left out
of both charts by unchecking *Show in Org Chart* in the HR settings of their
form, e.g. for a technical or service account.
